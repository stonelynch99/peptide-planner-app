Can I open EZPep like a phone app?

Yes. In the planner, open More and choose “Add EZPep to Home Screen” for the steps for your phone. Once added, open EZPep from its Home Screen icon.

You can also open the planner in your desktop browser. Sign in with the same approved account and check cloud status before transferring your saved planner to another device.

Open EZPep Planner
How do I sign in or set a password?

Use the email address approved for your beta account. Sign in with your email and password. If you previously used email codes, choose “Use an email code / set up a password” on the account screen. Keep the same account—you don’t need to register again.

Open your website account
What if I forget my password?

Enter your account email on the sign-in screen and select “Forgot password?”. Open the reset email and follow the secure steps. Check spam if it does not arrive. Never share your password, sign-in code or recovery link in feedback.

How does cloud saving work?

The planner keeps a local copy on your device. With cloud backup enabled, daily saving and catch-up when you reopen keep cloud-saved information up to date. Check the app’s cloud status before changing devices; changes that have not reached the cloud cannot be restored from a cloud backup.

Keep the app open if it shows a save or sync problem. Use its recovery and conflict guidance rather than clearing browser storage.

How do I set up reminders?

Open More → Notifications. Review reminder availability, plan settings and device setup. Enrollment is limited during the beta. On iPhone, use Safari to add EZPep to your Home Screen, then open it from that icon.

Reminders are a beta feature. Delivery depends on device and browser settings; check schedule coverage in the app and do not rely on a notification as your only record.

How can I report a problem?

Use the feedback form inside EZPep. Include what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. Add a screenshot only if you want to share it, and review it for private information first.

Wait for “Feedback sent successfully.” If an attachment is still pending, keep the report open and use the retry guidance.

What do dose, volume and syringe units mean?

Dose is the quantity of peptide recorded for a scheduled event, in mg or mcg. Volume is the liquid quantity in mL. Syringe units describe the draw on the selected syringe. These values are related by your vial and diluent inputs, but they are not interchangeable.

Professor Lynch’s question-mark help explains the fields inside the planner. Review the selected units and inputs before saving; EZPep does not choose a dose for you.

Where do I find the courses?

Open the planner, choose Learn, then Courses. The current beta includes three introductory paths with 15 short readings. Select a course and open a lesson to read it.

Mark lessons complete and use Continue learning to return to the next unread lesson. Progress is saved on this device for your signed-in account; it does not sync between devices. Full course content and membership access rules are still being prepared.

Open Planner → Learn → Courses
